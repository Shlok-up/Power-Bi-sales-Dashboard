🛒 Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

📊 Project Overview

The Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a comprehensive overview of e-commerce sales performance, helping businesses understand their profitability, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and payment preferences.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights through interactive charts, KPI cards, and filters.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Identify the most profitable product sub-categories.
* Understand quantity distribution across product categories.
* Analyze customer payment preferences.
* Track monthly profit trends.
* Provide an interactive view of business performance to support data-driven decision-making.

📈 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators (KPIs):

KPI	Value
Total Profit	37K
Total Quantity Sold	6K
Total Sales Amount	438K

Key Visualizations

1. Profit by Sub-Category

* Compares profitability across different product sub-categories.
* Helps identify high-performing and low-performing products.

2. Quantity by Category

* Displays the distribution of quantities sold across categories.
* Helps understand product category demand.

3. Quantity by Payment Mode

* Analyzes customer payment preferences.
* Includes COD, UPI, debit card, credit card, and EMI.

4. Profit by Month

* Shows monthly profit trends throughout the year.
* Helps identify profitable and less profitable periods.

5. Interactive Filters

* Quarter-based filtering.
* Additional slicer-based data exploration.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualization.
* Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.
* DAX – Calculations and measures.
* Data Visualization – KPI cards, bar charts, and donut charts.

💡 Key Business Insights

* The dashboard reports approximately 438K in total sales and 37K in total profit.
* Clothing contributes the largest share of total quantity sold.
* COD represents the highest share of payment-mode quantity.
* Monthly profit analysis highlights variations in business performance.
* Sub-category analysis helps identify products contributing more significantly to profitability.
